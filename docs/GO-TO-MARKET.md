# TopKaun — Go-To-Market Playbook

**"India ka #1 kaun?"**
A launch playbook you can actually execute from. Written for a non-technical, first-time founder in Ahmedabad.

---

## 0. First, say what we are (and are not) in one line

TopKaun is a **paid promotion and visibility service**. A business pays to appear high on a public leaderboard. Higher payment = higher rank = more eyeballs. The business always receives a **definite service**: a placement on the board, a shareable "We're #1 on TopKaun" card, and an embeddable badge for their website.

**Say it like this (customer-facing):**
> "Buy promotion. Climb the board. Show everyone you're #1 in your city/category."

**Words we always use:** promotion, advertising, visibility, placement, boost, top-up, listing, badge.
**Words we never use:** bet, stake, gamble, prize, jackpot, winnings, odds, pot, "win money."

**Why this matters:** The buyer never gets money back and never "wins" cash. They buy ad placement, the same idea as paying more for a bigger hoarding or a front-page newspaper ad. Keeping this framing in every message, invoice, and social post protects you legally and keeps the product honestly described. Put a plain line in the footer of the site and every invoice: *"TopKaun is a paid advertising and promotion service. Payments buy listing placement and visibility only. No cash prizes, no refunds of placement spend."*

## 1. The core growth loop

This is the engine. If it spins, you grow for almost free. If it doesn't, nothing else matters.

```
   (1) A business PAYS to get on / climb the board
                 │
                 ▼
   (2) Their rank changes → the board visibly moves (drama)
                 │
                 ▼
   (3) They get a "We're #1 / We're rising on TopKaun" card + badge
                 │
                 ▼
   (4) They SHARE it (pride) on WhatsApp / Instagram / their website
                 │
                 ▼
   (5) New people see it → visit TopKaun → see the live board
                 │
                 ▼
   (6) A rival / another business sees they're beaten → PAYS to climb
                 │
                 └──────────────► back to (1)
```

**The loop in one sentence:** A paid listing creates visible drama → the business proudly shares a card → new visitors arrive → some of them (especially rivals) pay to climb → more drama.

### What has to be TRUE for the loop to spin

| # | Must be true | How you make it true |
|---|---|---|
| A | There is a **real rival** on the board, not just one lonely listing | Seed boards with 20–30 listings before promoting them (Section 2). A board with 1 name has no drama. |
| B | Being outranked **feels bad enough to act** | Email + WhatsApp alert the instant someone is passed: *"You just dropped to #3 in Ahmedabad D2C."* |
| C | The share card is **genuinely brag-worthy** | Clean, good-looking card with their logo, rank, city/category. People only share things that make them look good. |
| D | A new visitor **immediately understands** what they're looking at | Board page must explain "higher pay = higher rank, buy your spot" in 5 seconds. |
| E | Paying again is **fast and cheap to start** | ₹199 minimum, UPI one-tap via Razorpay, "+₹100 to pass #1" button right there. |

**If any one of A–E is false, the loop stalls.** Your job in the first 6 weeks is to make all five true on ONE board.

## 2. The cold-start problem (the hardest part) and how to solve it

**The problem:** An empty leaderboard is worthless. No one pays to be "#1 of nobody." You must *manually* create the first market by hand, one business at a time, before any loop can spin.

**The solution: pick ONE beachhead and flood it.** Do not launch "All India." Do not launch 8 cities. Win one small board so completely that it looks alive, then expand.

### Recommended beachhead

> **Ahmedabad + D2C / local brands & agencies-creators.**

Why this one:
- You live in Ahmedabad. You can meet these people in person, which closes deals far faster than cold internet outreach.
- D2C brands, marketing agencies, and creators **already pay for promotion** (Instagram ads, influencers). TopKaun is just another promo channel they understand instantly.
- These people are **status-driven and online**. They will share a "#1" card. A sleepy B2B manufacturer will not.
- Ahmedabad vs Surat is a natural, fun **City War** rivalry you can lean on for content.

Run the **"Today" board for Ahmedabad D2C** as your very first live board. It resets at midnight IST, so being #1 is cheap (as low as ₹199) and low-risk for a first-timer to try. It gives you fresh drama every single day.

### Target: 20–30 real listings in the first 3 weeks

### Who to approach (in priority order)

| Priority | Who | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | People you **already know** — founder friends, agency owners, creators in Ahmedabad | Your own phone contacts, WhatsApp, past clients |
| 2 | Ahmedabad D2C brands | Instagram (search `#ahmedabadbusiness`, `#madeingujarat`, `#ahmedabadbrand`), local D2C stores on Instagram shopping |
| 3 | Marketing agencies & freelance creators | Instagram, LinkedIn (search "digital marketing Ahmedabad"), local co-working spaces (e.g. shared workspaces, startup hubs) |
| 4 | Local startup & founder communities | Startup WhatsApp/Telegram groups, TiE Ahmedabad events, college e-cells, D2C founder communities |
| 5 | Vendors at local markets / exhibitions | Pop-up markets, Sunday markets, trade expos in Ahmedabad |

**Tactic:** For the first 20, go for people you can reach directly or through one introduction. Warm beats cold 10-to-1 at this stage.

### The offer for the first 100 listings ("Founding Members")

Make early sign-ups feel special and low-risk:

1. **Founding Member badge** — a permanent badge on their listing + card (first 100 only). Scarcity + status. Say "Only 100 ever."
2. **Free initial boost credit** — e.g. you gift them ₹199–₹500 of placement credit so they experience being on the board **without paying first**. This removes the "why should I be the first guinea pig" fear. (You are funding this yourself; treat it as marketing spend. Budget maybe ₹10,000–₹20,000 of gifted credit across your first 30–50 listings.)
3. **Free design help** — you make their first social card and website badge for them, so they look good with zero effort.
4. **No long-term commitment** — "Try the Today board for ₹199. If you're not on it tomorrow, you've lost nothing but the price of two chai."

> Note on gifted credit: keep it clearly framed as a **promotional discount / free trial of an advertising service** — not as free money to gamble with. It buys placement, nothing else.

## 3. Launch timeline — first 6 weeks

Assume you are mostly solo. Keep it realistic.

| Week | Goal | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 0 (prep)** | Product + framing ready | Finalize the Ahmedabad D2C "Today" board. Test Razorpay end-to-end (UPI, card, netbanking) with a ₹1 test. Confirm GST invoice generates. Make 3–5 sample share cards. Write the "what is this" explainer on the board page. Draft your contact list of 50 target businesses. |
| **Week 1** | Seed the board privately | Reach out to your 10–15 warmest contacts (WhatsApp/in-person). Gift free credit, make their cards for them. Target: **10 live listings.** Do NOT promote publicly yet — an empty-looking board kills first impressions. |
| **Week 2** | Fill the board + spark first rivalry | Reach the next 15–20 from your list. Get to **20–30 listings.** Manually nudge two rivals: "X just passed you, want me to bump you back up?" Capture the first real "I got outranked and paid again" moment — this proves the loop. |
| **Week 3** | Go public (soft) | Now the board looks alive, start sharing publicly. Post the board + a City War angle (Ahmedabad vs Surat) on your own socials. Ask every listed business to share their card. Target: **first 5 organic/unprompted sign-ups** (people who found it, not from your list). |
| **Week 4** | Push virality | Run the City War as content daily. Line up 2–3 Ahmedabad micro-influencers / local creators to post. Pitch one local-news / local-startup newsletter angle. Launch the Hype (free upvote) feature publicly to pull in non-paying traffic. |
| **Week 5** | Add a second slice | If Ahmedabad D2C is spinning, open ONE adjacent board: either **Surat D2C** (fuels City War) or **Ahmedabad Agencies/Creators** (adjacent audience). Reuse the same seeding playbook. |
| **Week 6** | Review & decide | Check the weekly metrics (Section 6). Is the loop spinning without you pushing every listing? Decide: double down, fix the weak link (A–E in Section 1), or rethink the beachhead. |

**Golden rule:** Do not open a new board until the current one looks alive and has repeat payers. Width is the enemy early; depth is the friend.

## 4. Pricing & psychology

### Why ₹199 minimum
- **Low enough to be an impulse** — cheaper than a pizza. "Why not, let's try" money. Removes the fear of a first purchase.
- **High enough to be real** — people value what they pay for. A ₹10 listing feels like spam; ₹199 feels like a real ad buy.
- **Covers payment friction** — Razorpay's ~2% fee on ₹199 is a few rupees; margins stay healthy.

### Why the "Today" board matters
- It **resets at midnight IST**, so #1 is winnable cheaply every day. That means **daily drama** and a daily reason to come back.
- It's the **perfect first purchase**: "Be #1 in Ahmedabad for ₹199, just for today." Low commitment, instant gratification.
- It creates a **habit**: businesses check each morning, see they slipped off, and top up again. Daily reset = daily loop.

### How to encourage repeat top-ups — ethically
Do's (honest nudges):
- **Instant outranked alert:** "You dropped to #4 in Ahmedabad D2C." Factual, timely, useful. People act on real status loss.
- **Show the exact cost to climb:** "+₹100 to pass #1" button. Remove guesswork.
- **Celebrate wins:** when they hit #1, auto-send the share card. Pride fuels repeat buying.
- **Weekly recap:** "You were #1 for 3 days this week. Here's your card to share."

Don'ts (keep trust, avoid dark patterns):
- Don't fake rivals or fake rank drops. If a business ever learns a "rival" was fake, you're dead. Real drama only.
- Don't hide the total spend. Always show them what they've paid and what it bought (placement days, impressions).
- Don't pressure with false scarcity beyond the genuine Founding Member cap.
- Always allow them to simply stop. No auto-charges without clear consent.

### Keeping boards from going "dead" when one payer dominates
This is a real failure mode: one business pays ₹50,000, no one can catch them, everyone else gives up, board dies.

Fixes:
1. **The "Today" board is your main weapon** — it resets daily, so no one can permanently own it. Push most activity here.
2. **Many small boards, not one big one** — slice by city AND category AND "Today." A business dominating "All-India SaaS" still has "Ahmedabad SaaS" and "SaaS Today" to fight on. Everyone can be #1 *somewhere*.
3. **Rank bands / tiers in display** — celebrate "Top 3" and "Top 10" and "Rising fast," not only #1. Give the #4 business a shareable "Top 5 in Ahmedabad D2C" card so they still feel a win.
4. **Hype upvotes** as a free, non-money axis — a "Most Hyped" sort lets a loved small brand shine without outspending the big one. Keeps small players engaged.
5. **Watch for a runaway #1** as a weekly metric. If one listing owns a board untouched for 7+ days, that board is going stale — nudge challengers or spin up a fresh "Today"/sub-category angle.

## 5. Virality tactics specific to India

### WhatsApp sharing (your #1 channel — treat it as the hero)
- Every card has a big **"Share on WhatsApp"** button that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message + link. One tap.
- Pre-filled text in Hinglish: *"Hum TopKaun pe Ahmedabad me #1 hain! 🏆 Dekho 👉 [link]"*
- Make the share image the right shape for a **WhatsApp status** (vertical/square) — status views are huge reach in India.
- Give businesses a **"challenge a friend"** share: "Beat me on TopKaun if you can 😏" — turns sharing into a dare.

### City War as content
- The Ahmedabad vs Surat rivalry is **ready-made content**. Post a daily/weekly scoreboard: "Ahmedabad 62% vs Surat 38% — Surat, where you at? 👀"
- Tag local pages, use city hashtags (`#amdavad`, `#suratcity`). Civic pride drives clicks and shares in India like little else.
- Let each listing show "contributing to Ahmedabad" — so paying feels like repping your city, not just self-promo.

### Local-language angle (Gujarati / Hindi)
- You're in Gujarat — **Gujarati** cards and taglines will massively out-perform English here. Offer card text in Gujarati, Hindi, and English.
- Example Gujarati tagline on a card: *"અમદાવાદમાં નંબર ૧!"* (Number 1 in Ahmedabad!). Hindi: *"अहमदाबाद में नंबर 1!"*
- Local language = feels local, trustworthy, and shareable to family/city WhatsApp groups.

### Micro-influencers & local creators
- Don't chase big influencers. Target **Ahmedabad micro-creators (5k–50k followers)** — food pages, "things to do in Ahmedabad" pages, local business reels.
- Offer them a **free founding listing + their own "Creators" board** so *they* compete too. Creators competing for #1 is free content and recurring payment.
- Let them post the City War scoreboard — it's fun, native content their audience actually enjoys.

### Local news & startup angle
- Pitch a simple story to local/startup media: *"Ahmedabad founder builds a live leaderboard where businesses compete to be #1 — and a city-vs-city rivalry with Surat is heating up."* The City War is the hook journalists like.
- Target Gujarati newspapers' digital/startup sections, regional startup newsletters, and local business WhatsApp/Telegram groups.

## 6. Key metrics to watch weekly

Track these every Monday on one simple sheet. Rough targets are for the Ahmedabad beachhead in the first ~6 weeks.

| Metric | What it tells you | Healthy | Warning / failing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live paying listings (on your main board)** | Is the market real? | 20+ by Wk2, growing | Stuck below ~15, flat |
| **New listings this week** | Are you still acquiring? | 5–10+ | 0–2 |
| **Repeat top-ups this week** (listings that paid again) | Is the loop actually spinning? ⭐ most important | 30%+ of active listings top up again | Almost no one pays twice |
| **Revenue this week (₹)** | The business, simply | Growing week on week | Flat or shrinking |
| **Organic / unprompted sign-ups** | Is virality starting? | 3–5+ by Wk4 | Still 100% you hand-recruiting at Wk5 |
| **Card shares this week** | Is the loop's "share" step working? | Rising; most #1s share | Nobody shares their card |
| **Visitors → listing conversion** | Does the board sell itself? | A few % of visitors start a listing | Lots of visitors, near-zero sign-ups |
| **Outranked-alert → return-pay rate** | Does losing rank make people pay? | A meaningful share come back and top up | Alerts ignored |
| **Days any one listing owns a board untouched** | Board going stale? | ≤ 3–4 days | 7+ days (runaway #1, dead board) |

**The single number that matters most: repeat top-ups.** One-time buyers mean you have a novelty, not a loop. Repeat payers mean you have a business.

## 7. Honest risks & how to de-risk each

| Risk | What it looks like | How to de-risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Novelty decay** | Fun for 2 weeks, then everyone loses interest and stops paying. | Lean on the **daily "Today" reset** for recurring reasons to return. Keep adding fresh boards/angles (new categories, City War events). Build **habit via morning alerts**. Watch repeat-top-up rate weekly; if it drops, novelty is fading — add a new hook. |
| **Dead boards (runaway #1)** | One big payer owns a board, everyone else quits. | Many small boards + "Today" resets + Top-3/Top-10 cards + Hype axis (see Section 4). Monitor "days one listing owns a board." |
| **Trust / credibility of "#1 = just paid"** | People sneer: "That's not really #1, they just paid most." | **Own it, don't hide it.** Label clearly: "Promoted ranking — brands climb by boosting their promotion." Position it as honest advertising (like a sponsored slot), not a merit award. Add the **free Hype upvotes** as a *people's-choice* axis so there's a genuine popularity signal alongside the paid one. Transparency is the credibility. |
| **A business feels ripped off** | "I paid ₹5,000 and got nothing." | Set expectations up front: they're buying **placement + a card + a badge + impressions**, which they always receive — never "winning." Show them their impressions/views so value is visible. Offer the **free trial first** so they see it before spending real money. Clear footer + invoice language. No hidden auto-charges. |
| **Legal / payment framing** | Razorpay or someone flags it as gambling. | Consistent advertising framing everywhere (Section 0). Definite service always delivered, no cash returned, no chance-based outcome — the buyer fully controls their rank by how much promotion they buy. If ever unsure, get a quick opinion from a local lawyer/CA; GST invoices for an advertising service are standard and clean. |
| **Fake / spam / offensive listings** | Someone lists something inappropriate to be "#1". | Manual approval of new listings in the early days (you only have ~30 — review each). Simple reporting + takedown. Keep a short content policy. |
| **Seeding never catches (cold start fails)** | You hand-recruit 30 but no loop forms, no repeats. | That IS your Section 8 signal. Don't keep pouring free credit forever. Give it the honest 90-day test below. |

## 8. The 90-day test: "keep going" vs "pivot or stop"

Be honest with yourself at day 90. Judge the **Ahmedabad beachhead**, not a fantasy of all-India.

### ✅ "This is working — keep going" if MOST of these are true:
- **Repeat top-ups are real:** a meaningful chunk of listings (say **30%+**) pay more than once, without you personally pushing each one.
- **The loop spins semi-on-its-own:** you're getting **organic sign-ups** (people who found it via a shared card, not your contact list) — ideally several per week by month 3.
- **Revenue grows month over month** (even if small). Month 3 clearly ahead of Month 1.
- At least **one board stays genuinely competitive** (rank changes hands regularly, not one frozen #1).
- People **share their cards unprompted**.
- You can point to a handful of businesses who'd be **genuinely annoyed if TopKaun disappeared**.

→ If so: double down on Ahmedabad, then expand to the next city/category using the exact same seeding playbook.

### 🔁 "Pivot" if:
- People enjoy it but **won't pay repeatedly** → the engagement is real but the money model is wrong. Consider: flat promoted-listing subscriptions, or charging agencies/creators who clearly value visibility, while keeping the board free to view.
- One niche (e.g. creators, or restaurants, or a single city) is **on fire** while the rest is flat → refocus entirely on that niche.
- The "#1 = paid" framing keeps causing trust pushback but the **Hype/popularity** side is loved → consider leading with popularity and making paid promotion the secondary layer.

### 🛑 "Stop (or rethink from scratch)" if, after a real 90-day effort:
- You're still **hand-recruiting every single listing** — zero organic growth.
- **Almost no one pays twice** — it's a one-time novelty, not a loop.
- Boards go dead within days of seeding and **don't revive** despite the fixes in Section 4.
- You're spending more on **free credit / your own time** than any plausible path to revenue justifies.

**The one-line gut check at day 90:**
> *Are businesses paying again, and are strangers showing up because someone shared a card? If both are yes, you have a business. If both are no, you have a demo.*
